photo1.jpg ... photo8.jpg  -> your 8 pictures (jpg / jpeg / png / webp all work, just keep the names)

Tips
- Phone photos can be huge. Resize each one to ~1200px wide (or run them through squoosh.app)
  so the page loads fast on her phone.
- iPhone .HEIC photos don't show in browsers - export/convert them to .jpg first.
- Keep the mp3 under ~5-6 MB (128 kbps is plenty).
- Until you add them, pretty placeholders are shown and the site is silent.
